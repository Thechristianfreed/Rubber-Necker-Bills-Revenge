# Level and story ideas

A running list of ideas from the family, and which of them are built.

## Bonus levels

### The locker
The true story: in high school, Bill and his friends were at a friend's house
talking about a guy who bullied them. One friend left the room, came back, and
tossed a SKIPPY peanut butter jar at them. The jar turned out to be filled with
a turd. They went to the school and broke into the bully's locker. He played
tennis, so they took his racquet; one of them lobbed the turd out of the jar
and another smacked it with the racquet, launching bits through the strings.
They also closed some of it inside his history book. The next day a teacher
angrily asked why he did not have his book, and he replied: "Someone broke into
my locker and smushed chocolate milkshake in my book."

Built, as the bonus level **The SKIPPY Jar** (`src/scenes/LockerScene.ts`):

- The view is over Bill's shoulder, on foot, looking across the hallway at
  the bully's open locker.
- SKIPPY jars and chocolate milkshakes come out of the locker straight at the
  camera, as high lobs, flat drives and ones that bounce off the floor. Tap
  when one reaches the ring to send it back with the tennis racquet: a
  perfectly timed hit goes back into the locker, the rest splat on the wall,
  and some of it goes through the strings.
- His history book comes out too: hold DUCK, or it costs a heart.
- The racquet's special is GRAND SLAM: everything in the air goes back at once.
- After a win, a page of notebook paper tells how it ended: the teacher, and
  the "chocolate milkshake" line.

Not built yet:

- Slamming the history book shut as a finale.

### The Hot Carl
A true story from a high school sleepover. DJ fell asleep early (this was a
mistake). Ol' Poop-Sock-Neil came back from the bathroom with a dangling tube
sock containing one of his turds, and microwaved it, turning the weapon into
what he called a "Hot Carl". He tiptoed downstairs with the rest of the boys
who were still awake, crept up to DJ, and signalled for Christian to flip on
the light switch; then he blapped and bopped DJ on the head with it. Forrest
filmed the whole ordeal on his phone. The boys sprinted out of the room the
moment the light went off.

Built, as the bonus level **The Hot Carl** (`src/scenes/SleepoverScene.ts`):

- A page of notebook paper tells the story so far, then the room is dark.
- When Christian flips the light on, every tap is another bop on DJ's head.
  The score is how many you land before the light goes off. Eight spells of
  light, of different lengths, one of them a flicker.
- When the light is off, freeze. A swing in the dark hits the lamp and costs
  one of three hearts (one per spell of darkness at most).
- After the last light, or the third lamp, Alex's dad comes through the door
  and it is over: DJ wakes up and the boys run for it.

Not built yet:

- Photos (prompts HC1 to HC6 in `docs/art-prompts.md`); the room and the cast
  are cut paper for now.
- Recorded lines: Neil's "HOT CARL!" and whatever Alex's dad really said.

### The late teacher
Bill had a teacher who would freak out if anyone was late and lock the door so
they could not get into class. One day the teacher herself was late. Panicked
and flustered, she apologised: "Sorry, I got rear-ended." Bill, snidely:
"Do you mean in your car?"

- Possible shape: a school-era bonus stage where Bill has to beat the bell
  before the door locks, ending on the teacher arriving late and the line.
